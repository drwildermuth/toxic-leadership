Which monster gets under your skin?

A two-part reflection on toxic leadership. About 8 minutes.

Part 1 asks what drives you craziest in a leader. You pick 5 behaviors and rank them.

Part 2 asks how often you do a bit of the same things yourself. Almost everyone does, now and then. That is the point.

At the end you will see the two side by side. We will pause between the parts, so please keep this tab open.

Your answers stay on your own device. Nothing is sent or collected. This is a reflection tool, not a validated assessment.

    One thing to try this week

    Ask someone you work with: "What is one thing I do when I'm stressed that makes your job harder?" Then say thank you, and do not defend yourself.

    Non-toxic leaders do not wait for feedback. They ask for it.

    Behaviors are drawn from themes in Igbokwe et al., "Monsters, Inc.: Toxic Leadership and Engagement." This is a conversation starter built on a small qualitative study, not a diagnostic tool. Everyone has toxicity moments. The point is to notice them.
